2027 Finance Planner

Inputs are in GH₵ unless marked US$; the "Show" switch converts results to US$ at your exchange rate. Everything saves in this browser only. Projections are illustrations, not advice or guaranteed returns.

1. Income & tax

PAYE uses the GRA monthly bands for the 2026 year of assessment (in force from 1 Sep 2026), applied to gross minus employee SSNIT, for a Ghana-resident employee paid as basic salary. 2027 bands may differ, and paying a resident employee directly in US$ has legal limits, so confirm the payroll set-up with a Ghanaian payroll professional.

5. Car affordability

Defaults come from the Changan CS85 import listing discussed in the chat (about GH₵110,000 CIF plus GH₵40,000–50,000 duties). Those are advertised estimates, not quotes: get a written landed-cost quotation and a bank quotation showing the interest rate, fees and total repayment. Loan payments use standard reducing-balance amortisation.

6. Financial independence roadmap

Gross salary by year (US$ / month)

Results are in today's money: the return is converted to a real return using the inflation rate (0% inflation reproduces the nominal figures in the chat). When the emergency fund reaches its target, its monthly contribution moves into investments. Of each raise's extra take-home, the invested share goes to investments and the rest is assumed spent, which raises the financial-independence target. The target is annual spending divided by the withdrawal rate (4% = 25×). Spending counts every non-saving row, including rent, family support and travel.

7. Net worth tracker

Log your real balances each quarter and compare them with the plan. The plan line covers the three pots you are saving into (emergency fund, long-term investments, car fund) from the start month below, in nominal terms using the current scenario.

9. Scenarios

Every edit changes the active scenario only. Saving a copy creates a new scenario and switches to it, leaving the original untouched, so you can try something (a US$8,000 salary, a different car) and compare them side by side. Your net worth snapshots and monthly actuals are shared across scenarios.
